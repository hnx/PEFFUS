About

Overview

Peffus Network (“Peffus”) is a private organization serving as the umbrella entity for its subsidiary entities. Peffus oversees and supports a diverse portfolio of businesses, ventures, and organizations operating across various industries and areas of activity. Peffus provides strategic direction, administrative support, and shared resources to its subsidiaries while allowing each entity to operate independently within its respective domain.
